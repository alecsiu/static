How much do people in 24 countries like 34 world cuisines? Each number is the % of people who have tried a cuisine and say they like it (YouGov, May–Dec 2018). Explore it as a heatmap, by country, by cuisine, or head-to-head.

Source: YouGov cuisine popularity survey. Values were transcribed from the published chart; cells with a dashed outline were hard to read and were reconstructed so each row matches its published average, so treat them as estimates. Averages shown here are computed from the grid.
